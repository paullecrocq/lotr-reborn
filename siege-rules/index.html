Siege Base Rules

Every siege base is checked by the staff before it is validated. A base that breaks one of these rules is sent back for changes. Rules are numbered so you can quote them.

The path Gates Capture area Openings Checked automatically

The path

The path is the way the attackers walk from their spawn point (redstone block) to the capture area (emerald).

  1. P1

    A path must exist that connects the attackers' spawn point and the capture area.

  2. P2

    Only gates can block the path.

  3. P3

    At every location, the path must be at least 3 blocks wide.

  4. P4

    Lesser obstructions are tolerated next to the path, on the condition that the path is at least 5 blocks wide there. Lesser obstructions can slow players down.

  5. P5

    The path must not be next to any source of damage, for example lava or Morgul flowers.

  6. P6

    There must be no pit that prevents players from getting back to the path: every pit must have an exit.

Gates

Gates are LOTR gate blocks. Attackers break them with the siege ram.

  1. G1

    Every gate on the path must be at least 3×3.

  2. G2

    In front of each gate, there must be enough space to place a ram.

    The ram is set up on the attackers' side: it needs 6 free blocks in front of the gate, 2 blocks high, with 1 free block on each side (3 wide).

Capture area and spawns

  1. C1

    The capture area must be in a separate room from the defenders' spawn point.

Openings in walls and roofs

  1. H1

    Holes in walls aimed at the lower part of the attackers' bodies are not allowed.

  2. H2

    No hole in a wall or roof may be aimed directly at the capture area.

Checked automatically

The server refuses to submit or validate a base that doesn't meet these:

  • A diamond block (defenders' spawn), a redstone block (attackers' spawn) and emerald (capture area).
  • The defenders' spawn is at least 30 blocks from the capture area.
  • Every gate in the base stands upright and is at least 3×3.
  • Each gate has at most 200 HP, and all the gates together at most 1000 HP (/siegegate).

How a base gets validated

When the base is finished, the leader runs /faction siegebase <territory> submit. The staff checks it against these rules, then validates it or sends it back with a reason. /faction siegebase <territory> reopen unlocks a base for changes; it must then be validated again.
