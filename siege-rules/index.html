Siege Base Rules

Every siege base is checked by the staff before it is validated. A base that breaks one of these rules is sent back for changes. Rules are numbered so you can quote them.

The path Gates Capture area Openings During a siege Checked automatically

The path

The path is the way the attackers walk from their spawn point (redstone block) to the capture area (emerald).

  1. P1

    A path must exist that connects the attackers' spawn point and the capture area.

  2. P2

    Only gates can block the path.

  3. P3

    At every location, the path must be at least 3 blocks wide and 2 blocks high.

  4. P4

    The path must be free of any obstruction, including anything that slows players down or hurts them.

    Dart traps hurt players: they count as obstructions and can't be used on the path.

  5. P5

    The path must be continuous: players must be able to walk it back and forth without any obstruction. No parkour, no climbing, no drops.

    Staircases are allowed if each step is at most 1 block high for 1 block forward.

  6. P6

    Lesser obstructions are tolerated next to the path, never on it, and only where the path is at least 5 blocks wide.

  7. P7

    The path must not be next to any source of damage, for example lava or Morgul flowers.

  8. P8

    There must be no pit that prevents players from getting back to the path: every pit must have an exit.

  9. P9

    The attackers' spawn point must lead to the path. It can't be directly exposed to the defenders' area.

  10. P10

    Labyrinths are not allowed: the path must be clear and easy to follow.

Gates

Gates are LOTR gate blocks. Attackers break the gates on the path with the siege ram or with bombs.

  1. G1

    Every gate on the path must stand upright and be at least 3×3.

  2. G2

    In front of each gate on the path, there must be enough space to place a ram. Build the corridor so the ram fits.

    The ram is set up on the attackers' side: it needs 6 free blocks in front of the gate, 2 blocks high, with 1 free block on each side (3 wide).

  3. G3

    The HP of every gate on the path must be set with /siegegate: at most 200 HP per gate, and at most 1000 HP for all the gates on the path together.

  4. G4

    Only gates on the path may be set with /siegegate. You can use as many gates as you like elsewhere in your base; they aren't limited.

    The staff checks that no unset gate stands on the path.

Capture area and spawns

  1. C1

    The capture area is a 9×9 square centered on the emerald block (only one emerald block per base). From the emerald up, it must be free of any obstruction for at least 3 blocks of height.

  2. C2

    The capture area must be in a separate room from the defenders' spawn point, with no direct opening between the two rooms. Height counts: a spawn above or below the capture room follows the same rule.

  3. C3

    The capture area must be lit.

    The path doesn't have to be lit.

Openings in walls and roofs

  1. H1

    Holes in walls aimed at the lower part of the attackers' bodies are not allowed.

  2. H2

    With every gate in place and closed, no hole in a wall or roof may have a line of sight on the capture area.

    A line of sight that only opens once attackers have broken gates on the path is allowed.

During a siege

  • Defenders can open every gate, door and trapdoor of their base.
  • Attackers can't use anything in the base (gates, doors, chests, levers…). They break the gates on the path with the ram or with bombs.
  • The attackers capture by outnumbering the defenders inside the 9×9 capture area.

Checked automatically

The server refuses to submit or validate a base that doesn't meet these:

  • A diamond block (defenders' spawn), a redstone block (attackers' spawn) and exactly one emerald block (capture area).
  • The 9×9 capture area is free of blocks for 3 blocks above the emerald.
  • The defenders' spawn is at least 30 blocks from the capture area.
  • Every gate set with /siegegate stands upright and is at least 3×3.
  • The gates set with /siegegate have at most 200 HP each and 1000 HP together.

How a base gets validated

When the base is finished, the leader runs /faction siegebase <territory> submit. The staff checks it against these rules, then validates it or sends it back with a reason. /faction siegebase <territory> reopen unlocks a base for changes; it must then be validated again.
