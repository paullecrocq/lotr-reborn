LOTR Reborn Info Center

Everything the server adds on top of the LOTR Mod: how to earn LRX and coins, what abilities and rings do, how factions compete, go to war and rule territories, quests, the dungeon, the market, the PvP arena, the subscription, and the rules that keep it fair. First steps

You arrive in the lobby, a safe zone where nobody can fight or build.

  1. Pick a faction. See them all with /faction list, then join with /faction join <faction>. Your faction's leader (or a rank allowed to) can set a faction home you can teleport to.
  2. Head into Middle-earth. Play the LOTR Mod as usual. Killing NPCs, mining ores and harvesting crops earns you LRX.
  3. Claim your levels. When you level up, type /lrx claim to collect your coins and any ability unlocked.
  4. Spend wisely. Coins go to your vault. The lobby shops sell magic rings, ability uses and faction crafting tables.
  5. Look for quests. Some NPCs have quests for you: right-click them to talk.
  6. Come back any time with /lobby. The LOTR menu (press L) holds the map, fellowships and shield customisation.
Good to know: in Middle-earth you keep your inventory when you die. Use /home set and /home to save a spot and return to it.

Applying an ability

  1. Check your uses with /abilities.
  2. Hold the item, then type the ability's command, for example /unbreakable.
  3. Type the same command again within 15 seconds to confirm. One use is spent.
It cannot be undone. The ability stays on that item forever, and each item can carry each ability only once. The item's description shows its abilities.

Coins & shops

Coins are the server's currency. Your balance lives in a personal vault, safe from death and theft.

The vault

  • /vault shows your balance.
  • /vault deposit turns the coins in your inventory into balance. Coin items are worth 1, 10 and 100.
  • /vault withdraw <amount> gives you coin items back, for trading with other players.
  • /vault top lists the richest players.
  • /vault auto on deposits coins automatically as soon as they reach your inventory (picked up, sold, looted), with a chat message each time. Coins you were already carrying, or that you just withdrew, stay with you. /vault auto off stops it.

Level rewards, shop purchases, casino bets and secessions all use your vault balance directly.

Lobby shops

Right-click a shop sign in the lobby to buy; the price is shown on the sign in game and taken from your vault.

  • Magic rings. See Magic rings.
  • Ability uses. See Abilities.
  • Faction crafting tables, 27 of them (Gondor, Rohan, Mordor, Dwarven, High Elven, Hobbit, Umbar…).
  • Commander and smith spawn eggs: hire a faction commander, or set up your own smith. Bought by mistake? Hold the egg and type /eggreturn within 48 hours to get your coins back (only for eggs you bought yourself).

The market

/market (or /ah, /marketplace), or the market blocks in the lobby, open the market: every player offer, sorted, with a search. The Server shop page (/market shop) sells items from the server with unlimited stock.

  • Sell the stack in your hand: /market sell <price>. Listing costs 2% of the price, never refunded; the buyer pays the price and you receive all of it. Unsold items come back after 30 days.
  • Auction it: /market auction <starting price> <duration> (10m to 7d). Each bid is taken from the bidder's vault at once and refunded if outbid; the next bid must be at least 5% higher. A bid in the last minute adds a minute. The seller receives the final bid minus 5%.
  • /market buy <id>, /market bid <id> <amount>, /market cancel <id>, /market mine, /market search <words>.
  • Items and coins are delivered even if you are offline or your inventory is full; you get a summary when you log in.

Trading with a player

/trade <player> opens a trade window for both of you once they accept (/trade accept or the button). Click your items to offer them, add coins with the buttons, then both press Accept. Any change cancels the agreement, and closing the window, logging out or dying gives everyone their items back.

Rewards that don't fit in a full inventory are kept for you and delivered as soon as you free a slot. Nothing is dropped on the ground.

Factions

25 factions, from Gondor and Rohan to Mordor, Angmar and Umbar. Factions pool their LRX and compete every week and every month.

Joining and belonging

  • /faction list, then /faction join <faction>. You can only be in one faction; /faction leave to leave.
  • Any member can invite a player without a faction with /faction invite <player>; they join with one click.
  • /faction info [faction] shows a faction's leader, LRX and members, one per line (hover a name for their join date). /faction member <player> tells you where someone belongs. /faction banlist [faction] shows who is banned.
  • /faction home teleports you to your faction's home after 5 seconds; moving cancels it. Your mount comes with you. Your rank needs the home permission (given to every rank by default).
  • No leader? Any member can take the lead with /faction overtake.

Faction LRX, levy and contests

  • The leader (or a rank with the levy permission) sets a levy from 0 to 100% with /faction levy <value>. That share of the LRX each member earns goes to the faction's pool. Members can check the rate with /faction levy.
  • The pool decides two contests: a weekly one ending every Friday at 20:00 (server time), and a monthly one following the calendar month. The faction with the most LRX wins; on a tie, the first to reach it.
  • Every faction that collects 10,000 LRX in a week receives 5,000 coins, winner or not.
  • The winner collects the prize with /faction claim (or /faction reward): the leader, or a rank with the claim permission. The same command collects your territories' weekly resources.
  • Standings: /faction top week, /faction top month, /faction top lrx (all-time) and /faction top members.
  • Who gave the most: /faction contrib ranks your faction's members by the LRX they gave to the faction this week, or lastweek, month, lastmonth.

Faction PvP

Killing players of another faction only takes LRX from their pool when your factions are at war.

Wars & territories

24 territories, from Erebor and Minas Tirith to Barad Dur and Umbar, can be ruled by factions. Wars decide who keeps them.

Live map: see who rules what, every fee and every territory's resources on the conquest map (in game: /conquestmap).

Ruling a territory

  • /territory list (or /t list) shows every territory, its owner and its weekly fee, plus what your faction owes. /t info <territory> gives the details, /t faction [faction] what a faction rules.
  • Claim one with /t overtake <territory> <LRX> (or /t bid). Your bid becomes its weekly fee, paid from your faction's LRX pool. A free territory needs at least its base fee (500 LRX) and is yours at once; the first week is paid immediately.
  • Taking one from another faction: bid more than its current fee. The owner has 24 hours to keep it with /t match <territory>, which raises its fee to your bid +10%. If they don't, it changes hands and you pay your first week. A higher bid from another faction replaces yours. /t cancel <territory> withdraws your bid.
  • A held territory's fee never goes down. /t abandon <territory> gives it up (no refund; your faction can't take it back for 7 days).

Payday and resources

  • Every Sunday at 20:00 (server time) the pool pays one week for each territory. You're warned the day before if it can't cover everything.
  • When the pool runs short, territories the leader marked with /t keep <territory> are paid first, then the cheapest; the most expensive are lost first.
  • Each paid week adds the territory's resources to your faction's storage (up to 2 weeks waiting). Collect them with /faction claim: items and coins go to whoever claims, LRX tokens to the pool.

Siege bases and the 3-territory limit

Building a siege base? Read the Siege Base Rules first: the staff checks every base against them before validating it.
  • A faction can rule 3 territories, plus 3 more for every siege base validated by the staff: 6 with one base, 9 with two, and so on. A base still being built, or reopened for changes, doesn't count. Above your limit, the last territories in your payment order are lost, so get another base validated before reopening one.
  • A siege base is a fortress built in the build world and tied to one territory; ask the staff to create one. The leader invites builders with /plot invite; they find it with /plot find <territory>. Only members of the faction holding the territory can enter it.
  • When it's finished, the leader submits it with /faction siegebase <territory> submit; the staff checks it, and once validated it can be besieged. /faction siegebase <territory> reopen unlocks it for changes.
  • What a base needs: a diamond block (defenders' spawn), a redstone block (attackers' spawn) and emerald (the capture area). The defenders' spawn must be at least 30 blocks from the capture area, and not in the same room.
  • Gates are LOTR gate blocks. Every gate must stand upright and be at least 3x3, so a ram can break it. Builders set each gate's health with /siegegate (look at the gate): up to 200 HP per gate and 1000 HP for all the gates of the base. /siegegate list shows them all.
  • The floor is 5 blocks thick: dig trenches and holes as you like, but the bottom layer can never be broken.

War

  • A leader declares war with /faction war declare <faction>. Both factions need at least 2,000 LRX in their pool. Fighting starts 24 hours later.
  • During a war, killing an enemy takes 1% of their faction's pool for yours (2% for their leader, at least 10 LRX). The same player can only be robbed once every 10 minutes, and never in safe zones, the PvP arena, events or the build world.
  • A faction whose pool falls below 1,000 LRX loses the war. The winner takes every territory the loser held.
  • Leaders can negotiate with /faction war armistice: LRX, coins and territories in both directions. Offers last 48 hours; signing ends the war.
  • After a war, the same two factions can't fight again for 7 days. /faction war info <faction> shows any faction's wars.

Sieges

  • During a war, the leader (or a rank with all) can besiege an enemy territory whose base is validated: /faction war siege <faction> <territory>. The attacker proposes weekend times, the defender picks one within 3 days.
  • Registration opens 15 minutes before, for the two factions only: stand in the event area until the start. Only registered players fight; nobody can join once it has started. /siege lists the planned sieges.
  • The siege base is copied into the PvP arena and the battle starts at once. Bring your own gear: you keep it when you die. Leaving the arena takes you out of the siege for good.
  • The goal: the attackers must hold the capture area (outnumber the defenders on it) for 500 seconds in total within 45 minutes. Being pushed off pauses the count, it doesn't reset it. If time runs out, the defenders win.
  • Respawns: attackers come back at once; defenders wait 15 seconds at their spawn.
  • Siege equipment: the attackers' commander (their leader, or the highest rank present) receives the bombs, rams and torches and drops them to his players. Only attackers can pick them up.
  • Gates: attackers can't open them. Right-click a gate with the siege ram to set it up in front of it. It lies on the ground until at least half of the living attackers (2 minimum) stand next to it; then they lift it and it strikes every 2 seconds for 10 HP. When a gate reaches 0 HP, it falls.
  • Bombs: place a siege bomb, wait 5 seconds, then right-click it with a siege torch: it explodes a second later, breaking walls around it. Defenders can break a bomb before it is lit. Bombs never break gates, spawns, the capture area or the arena's floor and ceiling.

Secession & putsch

Members unhappy with their leader can break away and fight for the faction.

  1. Secession. A member of at least 2 weeks pays a fee (shown in game) and starts it with /faction secession. They become the separatist leader. Other members can join it with the same command.
  2. Separatists. Players outside any faction can join with /faction join <faction> separatists.
  3. Declaring the putsch. After 3 days, the separatist leader declares it with /faction putsch, proposing weekend dates.
  4. Picking the date. The faction leader has 3 days to choose one of the dates; if they don't, the earliest one is used.
  5. The fight. Registration opens 15 minutes before, in the event area, for that faction's members and its separatists only. Everyone keeps their inventory and has 5 respawns. Logging out eliminates you. The last side standing wins.
  6. The outcome. If the separatists win, their leader takes over the faction. If they lose, the secession ends. A faction can't face another secession for 14 days.

Teleports & homes

Teleport requests

  • /tpa <player> asks to go to them; /tpahere <player> asks them to come to you.
  • The other player accepts with /tpa, /tpaccept or the clickable button, or refuses with /tpdeny. Cancel your own with /tpacancel.
  • Requests expire after 20 seconds. The teleport happens 3 seconds after accepting; you can move meanwhile, but getting hit cancels it.
  • Your horse, warg or other mount comes along.

Homes

  • /home set saves your home; /home takes you back there, mount included.
  • You can't set a home inside a safe zone such as the lobby.
Combat lock. Hitting a player, or being hit by one, puts both of you in combat for 30 seconds: no /tpa, no /home, no /faction home, and pending teleports are cancelled. Don't log out while in combat.

Teleports never cross between the survival world and the build world; use /build for that.

Build world

A flat creative world for designing builds, with its own inventory.

  • /build takes you there and back. Your survival inventory is stored safely and given back when you return, at the exact spot you left.
  • Find a free plot with /plot find 1 (32×32), 2 (64×64) or 3 (128×128), then claim it with /plot create <name> <size>.
  • Visitors are welcome. Let friends build with /plot invite <plot> <player>, keep someone out with /plot ban, change the floor with /plot setground. See /plot help.
  • No mobs, TNT, fire or ender chests there, and nothing earns LRX or stats.

Quests

Some NPCs of Middle-earth have stories to tell and tasks to give.

  • Right-click a quest NPC to talk: one line per click. When they're done talking, your objective appears in chat.
  • Objectives can be to bring items, slay creatures, reach a place, find something hidden… Some quests only open once you've finished another.
  • Finishing a quest pays its reward. /quest shows your quests and current objectives, /quest done the ones you've finished, /quest abandon <quest> gives one up.
  • Sneak + right-click a quest NPC to trade with it as usual.

The dungeon

Beyond the Doors of Durin, halls full of orcs, cave trolls, and Durin's Bane waiting at the end. The doors only open for those who know the word.

PvP arena

Matches and duels in the arena, with kits handed out for the fight. Your own inventory is kept aside and given back afterwards.

Duels

/duel <player> challenges someone; they answer with /duel accept or /duel deny.

Matches

/pvp (or a [PvP] sign) opens a menu to start a match: pick the mode, the map and the goal. /pvp status shows the current match, /pvp leave leaves it.

ModeHow to win
Team deathmatchThe first team to reach the kill goal.
Free-for-allEvery player for themselves.
King of the HillHold the hill to score.
DominationTwo teams fight over one capture point at a time. Standing on it scores points; the point moves after a while, or once a team has taken enough points from it.
AssaultAttackers capture the points one after another; defenders hold until time runs out. Defenders respawn after a short delay, and an odd player joins the attackers.

In Domination and Assault, a compass and the sidebar show the direction and distance to the active point. Kills and deaths in arena matches don't count in /stats.

Matches can pay rewards to the winning team and to every participant. Matches started by players only pay with enough players and a real fight.

Casino

/casino takes you there. Right-click a table to sit; you play with buttons in the chat.

  • Bets from 10 to 100,000 coins, taken from your vault. Set your bet with /bet <amount>.
  • Blackjack, up to 5 players against the bank: win pays 1:1, blackjack pays 3:2, a tie returns your bet. The bank draws to 17. Six-deck shoe, no insurance. A pair can be split into two hands with [Split] (same bet again, up to 4 hands), played one after the other.
  • Roulette, European (single zero), up to 8 players: red/black, odd/even, 1–18/19–36 pay 1:1; dozens and columns pay 2:1; a single number pays 35:1.
  • Some tables can be watched without playing: /casino watch, sneak + right-click the table, or right-click a full table.
  • /casino leave leaves your table (or stops watching), /casino help shows the rules, /topcasinostats shows the biggest win, biggest loss and top gambler.
  • /cstats [player] shows a player's casino record; /cstats top loss|win|gamble the top 5.

Events & chat

Server events

Events are announced in chat with a start time. To register, stand in the event area until it starts; leaving the area unregisters you, even for a moment. /event shows the current event. Winners (and sometimes every participant) receive rewards, delivered even if their inventory is full.

Cosmetic armour

/cosmetic (/cos): hold an armour piece and type /cosmetic equip to store it as a cosmetic. Other players then see it instead of the real piece you wear in that slot (you must wear one). Your real armour still does all the work. /cosmetic remove <slot|all> gives it back, /cosmetic hide and show switch the look off and on. Mithril, galvorn and Morgul armour can't be cosmetics, and always show when worn.

Alignment reset

/alignmentreset sets your LOTR alignment back to 0 with every faction. It can't be undone: type /alignmentreset confirm within 30 seconds to apply it. You may lose your pledge.

Votes

/timevote start or /weathervote start opens a vote; everyone clicks [Yes] or [No] in chat. The vote ends early once a majority of the active players has answered.

Team chats

Private group chats: /teamchat create <name>, invite with /teamchat invite <player>, and /teamchat (or /tc) to switch between global chat and your team.

AFK

  • /afk: stand still for 5 seconds and you are marked AFK. After 10 minutes without moving you become AFK automatically.
  • AFK players are sent to the AFK area and shown as [AFK] in the Tab list. Moving, looking around or chatting brings you back where you were.
  • Time spent AFK doesn't count toward your play time in /stats.

Chat titles

Some players show a title before their name in chat: [Admin] and [Helper] for the staff, and subscriber titles. Hover a title to read it. /title lists yours, /title use <title> picks one, /title off hides it.

Stats

/stats [player] shows play time, kills, deaths and blocks; /stats top <stat> shows the leaders. Time spent AFK, PvP arena matches and staff in admin mode don't count.

Subscription

A monthly subscription supports the server and unlocks comfort and style features. Subscribe on the store with /store; /subscription (or /sub) shows your status and features.

  • Features are active as soon as the payment goes through, and you're reminded in game when fewer than 3 days are left.
  • If the subscription ends, your settings (nickname, messages, extra homes, titles) are kept and come back when you subscribe again.

Homes and travel

  • /home2 and /home3: two extra homes (/home2 set, /home3 set), with the same wait and combat lock as /home.
  • /cooldown (/cd): resets your LOTR fast-travel cooldown.

Chat and identity

  • /nick <name>: a coloured nickname in chat (3–16 letters, digits or _; colours like &6; _ shows as a space). Hovering it shows your real name. /nick off removes it.
  • /joinmsg <message> and /leavemsg <message>: your own message when you join or leave (60 characters, {name} = your name, colours allowed, off to remove).
  • Subscriber titles before your name in chat, unlocked by your total time subscribed (all subscriptions added up). Each rank has a good and an evil version; you choose which one to show:

Convenience

  • /hat: wear the item in your hand as a hat.
  • /crafting (/ct): a crafting table anywhere.
  • /rename <name>: rename the item in your hand with colours and styles (40 characters). /rename off restores the name. Quest, lore and event items can't be renamed.
  • These three don't work during arena matches.

Contributor's Almanac

  • A daily task, new every day at midnight (Paris time): bring the requested items to the Almanac in the lobby and right-click it. Partial deliveries count.
  • Each task has a rarity (Common, Uncommon, Rare, Legendary) with matching LRX and coin rewards; coins go to your vault, LRX goes through your faction's levy.
  • Streak bonus: each day completed in a row adds ×0.05 to your rewards, up to ×2 on day 21. Missing a day resets it.
  • /almanac shows today's task, your progress and your streak.
Subscriptions give comfort, style and recognition: no gear and no combat stats.

Rules & fair play

The full rules are in game with /rules. These are the ones that come up most.

Not allowed

  • X-ray, ore or cave locators, and minimaps that show players.
  • Autoclickers, macros and AFK farms.
  • World downloaders; Schematica printing while AFK, or copying someone's build without permission.
  • Block glitching, escaping the lobby, glitching through walls by dismounting, and anything that equips armour instantly.
  • Breaking a protected block to see behind it, shooting protected items with ranged weapons, or trampling banner-protected crops on purpose.

What the server checks automatically

  • Autoclicking: clicking too fast or with machine-like regularity is reported to the staff.
  • AFK farming: clicking for several minutes without moving or looking around sends you to the AFK area and alerts the staff.
  • X-ray: finding hidden ores far more often than normal mining allows is reported. Ores you find in caves don't count against you.
  • Barrow-wight farms: farms are allowed, but the server detects them. Each farm kill earns a warning; after 5, Barrow-wights killed in that farm no longer drop items.
Protect your land with a LOTR banner: place one and set up its protection so other players can't build, break or loot there.
